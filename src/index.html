Overprint Preview

Off: the page is drawn exactly as the PDF describes it — vectors and text as vectors (sharp at any zoom), images at their own pixels. On: shows what an overprint-aware press will produce; ink mixing has to be calculated per pixel, so this view (and Separations) is drawn at screen resolution.

Separations

  • Open a PDF to see its separations.

Hover over the page to read ink at the cursor; click to hold a reading, click again to release. Untick plates to inspect trapping — e.g. whether black text overprints (colours still underneath) or knocks out (a white gap in the other plates).

Fonts

  • Open a PDF to see its fonts.

Every font should be embedded for print. A font that isn't gets swapped for another on the printer, so text can change or reflow. Converting text to outlines removes the need for fonts.

Colour & Fonts

    These rewrite the working copy (use Save PDF as to save it). Images keep full resolution and existing CMYK values, overprint and page boxes are kept. Converting spot colours also converts any RGB, because both go through the same CMYK conversion.

    Recolour

    Turn on the Recolour tool (R) and click a shape or text on the page to pick its colours.

    Current fill
    Current stroke

    New colours are CMYK. "Same" options follow the Apply edits to setting (all pages or this page).

    Selected Objects

    Turn on the Select tool (V) and click a shape, text or image on the page.

    Drag the selection to move it; arrow keys nudge it 0.5 mm (Shift: 5 mm). These edits apply to this page only. A placed graphic, such as a logo placed in InDesign, moves as one piece.

    Page Rotation

    Rotates the page. Applies straight away and the preview and boxes update to match.

    Page Boxes

    Each side is measured in from the nearest edge of the page, as shown on screen. Edits apply to a working copy; your original file is untouched until you use Save PDF as.

        Mangoprint Prepress PDF

        Version 1.0.1
