Overprint Preview

Off: the page is drawn exactly as the PDF describes it — vectors and text as vectors (sharp at any zoom), images at their own pixels. On: shows what an overprint-aware press will produce; ink mixing has to be calculated per pixel, so this view (and Separations) is drawn at screen resolution.

Separations

  • Open a PDF to see its separations.

Hover over the page to read ink at the cursor; click to hold a reading, click again to release. Untick plates to inspect trapping — e.g. whether black text overprints (colours still underneath) or knocks out (a white gap in the other plates).

Colour & Fonts

    These rewrite the working copy (use Export PDF to save it). Images keep full resolution and existing CMYK values, overprint and page boxes are kept. Converting spot colours also converts any RGB, because both go through the same CMYK conversion.

    Page Rotation

    Works like Acrobat's Rotate Pages. Applies straight away and the preview and boxes update to match.

    Page Boxes

    Each side is measured in from the nearest edge of the page, as shown on screen. Edits apply to a working copy; your original file is untouched until you use Export PDF.

    Mangoprint Prepress PDF

    Version 0.1 beta

    A free tool for checking print-ready PDFs: overprint, separations, ink values, trim and bleed, RGB and spot colours.

    Developed and maintained by Chris Cork from Mangoprint.co.uk.

    Mangoprint Prepress PDF is free to use. Many coffees were consumed while building it, and many more still are. If it saves you time, buy Chris a coffee ☕

    Built with

    • Ghostscript by Artifex Software (AGPL-3.0): overprint preview, separations, ink readings and colour/font conversions
    • PDF.js by Mozilla (Apache-2.0): sharp vector page display and text selection
    • Tauri (MIT / Apache-2.0): the desktop app framework
    • lopdf (MIT): reading and editing page boxes and rotation
    • Poppins typeface (SIL Open Font License)
