Overprint Preview

Off: the page is drawn exactly as the PDF describes it — vectors and text as vectors (sharp at any zoom), images at their own pixels. On: shows what an overprint-aware press will produce; ink mixing has to be calculated per pixel, so this view (and Separations) is rendered by Ghostscript at screen resolution.

Separations

  • Open a PDF to see its separations.

Hover over the page to read ink at the cursor; click to hold a reading, click again to release. Untick plates to inspect trapping — e.g. whether black text overprints (colours still underneath) or knocks out (a white gap in the other plates).

Page Rotation

Sets the page's /Rotate entry — the same thing Acrobat's "Rotate Pages" changes. Applies immediately, and the preview + box overlay re-render to match.

Page Boxes

Measurements in mm, from the bottom-left corner of the unrotated page. Edits apply to a working copy; your original file is untouched until you use Export PDF.
